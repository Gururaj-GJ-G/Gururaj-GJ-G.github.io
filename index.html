01 — About

An investigator who also builds the tooling

Six years in fraud, risk and financial crime; the systems to match.

I investigate fraud — and I build the explainable tooling that helps investigations scale. My core experience comes from G2 Risk Solutions (Fintellix), where I ran multi-layered transaction-laundering and prohibited-merchant investigations across nine payment networks.

Before that I investigated cloud-account fraud at Amazon Web Services, ran regulatory-compliance and sanctions/PEP screening reviews for Google's digital payments at Accenture, and handled buyer-side payment fraud at Flipkart.

I also work as a merchant-risk advisor: the Zarelva Merchant Onboarding & Payment-Risk Playbook turns card-network compliance requirements into a 14-section, five-layer onboarding framework that payment facilitators, acquirers and fintech platforms can apply directly.

Every component I build is designed to be transparent, auditable and explainable, with the evidence behind each decision shown to the investigator.

Career note: from August 2025 to August 2026 I took a planned break as primary caregiver for a family member, continuing independent research throughout, then held a short-term customer-success role at Onsurity Technologies (Aug–Oct 2026). I am now returning full-time to fraud investigation and financial-crime work — my chosen career focus.
